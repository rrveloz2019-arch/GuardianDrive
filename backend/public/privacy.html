GuardianDrive Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

GuardianDrive is a hands-free voice assistant that helps you make calls, send texts, and manage email and calendar while driving. This page explains exactly what information GuardianDrive accesses, why, and how it's handled — in plain language, matching what the app actually does.

What GuardianDrive accesses, and why

DataWhyWhere it's stored
Google account: email, basic profile, Gmail (read & send), Calendar (read & write) Powers "read my email," "what's on my calendar," and similar voice commands Access is via a secure Google sign-in token stored on GuardianDrive's server. GuardianDrive does not store copies of your emails or calendar events — it fetches them live from Google when you ask.
Microsoft/Outlook account (optional): email, basic profile, Mail (read & send) Only if you choose to connect a secondary Outlook email address Same as above — a secure sign-in token on GuardianDrive's server, live fetch, no stored copies
Phone number and message text (SMS feature only) Sent to Twilio's API to deliver a real text message you asked GuardianDrive to send from your GuardianDrive number Passed through to Twilio to send; not stored by GuardianDrive afterward
Contacts you save in the app (name, optional alias, phone number) So a spoken command like "call my wife" can resolve to a real number Stored on GuardianDrive's server. Not synced from your phone's contacts, not shared with anyone
Assistant name and wake phrase you choose Personalizes how you address your assistant Stored on GuardianDrive's server
Voice command mapping preferences Which spoken phrases trigger which actions Stored on GuardianDrive's server, as text only
Voice training progress Tracks how many of the training phrases you've completed Stored as a count and status only. GuardianDrive does not currently record or store actual audio of your voice. If real voice-recognition training is added in the future, this policy will be updated before any audio is ever captured.

What GuardianDrive does not do

  • Does not sell your data to anyone, for any reason
  • Does not use your data for advertising or ad targeting
  • Does not track your behavior for analytics beyond what's needed to run the app
  • Does not see or store the content of calls, texts, WhatsApp messages, or social posts you send through your phone's own Phone/Messages/WhatsApp apps — GuardianDrive only prepares the link (who to contact, what to say) and your phone's own app handles the rest

Third-party services involved

Because GuardianDrive connects to your Google, Microsoft, and (for SMS) Twilio accounts on your behalf, each of those providers' own privacy policies also applies to how they handle the data GuardianDrive is authorized to access:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Twilio Privacy Policy

Your control over your data

  • You can disconnect your Google or Microsoft account at any time from your Google/Microsoft account security settings, which immediately revokes GuardianDrive's access.
  • You can delete any saved contact directly from the Contacts tab in the dashboard.
  • To request deletion of any other data GuardianDrive holds about you, contact us using the email below.
